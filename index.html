📋 How the pool works

  • Pick group match winners — Go to the Group Stage tab and pick the winner of each of the 72 matches. Complete all picks before the group stage deadline.
  • Knockout bracket opens after the group stage — Once real-world group results are in, the commissioner seeds the 32 advancing teams into the official bracket. You then pick round by round.
  • Pick knockout winners round by round — Each round opens when the commissioner unlocks it. Pick the winner of each match — your pick cascades forward automatically.
  • Scores update as results come in — The commissioner enters actual results. The leaderboard updates automatically for everyone.
  • Highest total score wins the pool.
