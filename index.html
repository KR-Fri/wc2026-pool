📋 How the pool works

  • Pick group match winners — Go to Group Stage and pick the winner of each of the 72 matches. Complete all picks before the group stage deadline.
  • Knockout bracket opens after group stage — Once real-world group results are in, the commissioner seeds the 32 advancing teams into the official bracket and opens it for picking.
  • Pick your full knockout bracket in one go — Pick the winner of every match from Round of 32 all the way to the Final, including the 3rd place match. Your picks cascade forward automatically as you select winners.
  • My Score shows your results — As the commissioner enters actual results, your picks highlight green (correct) or red (wrong). Points accumulate automatically.
  • Highest total score wins.
