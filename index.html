📋 How the pool works

  • Pick group match winners — Go to the Group Stage tab and click the team you think wins each of the 48 matches. You must complete all group picks before the group stage deadline.
  • Your knockout bracket auto-fills — Based on your group picks, the app figures out who advances to the Round of 32. Your implied bracket is saved automatically when the group stage locks.
  • Revise knockout picks when the round opens — The commissioner opens each knockout round after results are in. You can change your picks — but changing a pick costs you the 2× bonus for that match.
  • Scores update as results come in — The commissioner enters actual match results. The leaderboard updates automatically.
  • Highest score wins the pool.

⭐ The 2× Loyalty Bonus

When the group stage locks, the app takes a snapshot of your implied knockout bracket — the teams that would advance based on your group picks. Any knockout pick that matches your original snapshot earns 2× points if correct. If you change a knockout pick after the round opens, that pick earns only 1× points. It pays to trust your gut from the start.

🗓 Tournament timeline

  • Now until Group Stage Deadline: Make all your group stage picks
  • Group Stage Deadline: Picks lock, your knockout snapshot is saved
  • Commissioner opens each knockout round after real-world results are in
  • Knockout Deadline: All knockout picks lock, final scoring begins
